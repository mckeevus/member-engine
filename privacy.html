Privacy.

This notice explains what Member Engine collects through this website and what happens to it.

The short version: if you enter your email, it is used to respond to your enquiry about a win-back campaign. It is not published on GitHub or added to a general marketing list.

What is collected

The enquiry form collects the email address you provide. The website’s hosting and form providers may also process ordinary technical information such as your IP address, browser information and submission time for security and delivery.

Why it is collected

Your information is used to respond to your request, discuss whether a win-back campaign is suitable for your business and maintain a record of that conversation.

Who processes it

When the enquiry form is enabled, the website sends form submissions to Sender.net, which acts as the contact-management and email service provider. Cloudflare hosts and delivers the website. These providers may process limited technical or delivery information and may process information outside Australia.

How long it is kept

Enquiry information is kept only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage the business relationship and meet applicable legal or record-keeping requirements.

Your choices

You can ask to access, correct or delete the personal information Member Engine holds about you. Reply to any email you receive from Member Engine with your request. Any marketing email sent separately will identify the sender and include an unsubscribe option.

Former-member data

Do not send a member database through this website or ordinary email. If a campaign proceeds, Member Engine will agree on a secure transfer method and how the data may be used before receiving any former-member information.

Updates

This notice was last updated on 7 September 2026 and may be updated when the website or service providers change.
