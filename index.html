The opportunity

Your old member list isn’t dead.

It’s full of people who already know your business, trusted you enough to join and may only need the right reason to return. That makes them much easier to win back than a complete stranger.

The difference

Different members left for different reasons.

A useful win-back campaign does more than send the same discount to everyone. I use the information available in your member records to segment the list and match the message and comeback offer to the reason they left.

The member who left because of price should not receive the same offer as someone who was injured, changed shifts or simply fell out of the habit.

Your customer data

Your list stays yours.

The campaign is sent under your business name, using only former members you are permitted to contact. Wherever practical, it runs through your existing email or membership platform.
